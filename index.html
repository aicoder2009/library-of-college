How it works

Collect now. Retrieve at 11:58pm.

The best essay material never shows up while you're staring at the prompt. LoC splits the job in two so nothing gets lost in between.

1

Dump the story

Press d anywhere and brain-dump the moment — messy is fine. AI titles it and files it into the right categories for you.

2

Paste the prompt

Drop in any supplemental prompt. ✦ Match ranks your entries strong or possible with a concrete why — and Breakdown decodes what the prompt is secretly asking.

3

Write from evidence

Draft with receipts instead of a blank page. Luggage-tag chips track which school each story was spent on, so nothing gets reused by accident.

Features

Built for the way essays actually get written.

Frictionless capture

Quick-dump modal, per-category dump bars, and a slide-over composer. The whole point is that writing something down never feels like a task.

✦ Prompt matching

Paste a real prompt and get your best-fitting stories back, each with a reason. Breakdown adds school detection, a gap analysis, and a suggested outline.

Two-speed search

⌘K opens a palette with a literal Verbatim tab and an ✦ Ideas tab that searches by meaning — "that essay about losing" finds the chess story.

Used-in tracking

Every entry carries tags for the essays it's already been spent on, so you can see your coverage across schools at a glance.

A warm reading room

Serif reading view, paper palette, highlighter-stroke headings, full dark mode. It reads like a notebook, not a database.

Keyboard-first

d dump, n new, j/k review, ⌘K jump anywhere. Your hands never leave the keys.

Your data

Your stories are yours.

Private by default

Sign-in is allowlisted per person. Nobody else — including other users — can ever read your library.

Backed up daily

Server-side snapshots every day, point-in-time recovery, and wipe protection that rejects suspicious overwrites.

Leave anytime

One-click JSON export of everything you've written, whenever you want it. No lock-in, no formats to fight.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What exactly is Library of College?

A personal evidence bank for college applications. You collect real moments — stories, details, small scenes — all year, and when supplemental essays arrive, LoC surfaces which of your own stories fit each prompt. It helps you find your material; the writing stays yours.

Does the AI write my essays?

No, and it's designed not to. The AI only works on what you've already written: filing it, finding it, and matching it against prompts. What goes on the page is you.

Why is there a waitlist?

LoC started as one student's personal tool during application season, and it's being opened up carefully so it stays fast and personal. Joining the waitlist is the way in — we'll reach out as spots open.

What does it cost?

Nothing while it's in beta. If that ever changes, everyone on the waitlist hears about it first — and export is always free.

Is my writing used to train anything?

No. Your entries live in your private library and are only sent to an AI model at the moment you ask for a title, a match, or a search — never for training.
